Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about how we care for your companion? Find answers below.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes, we are fully insured for pet care services (including public liability and key holding) and certified in canine first aid to keep your pets totally safe.

How do I track my dog's walk?

For our 60-minute walks, we provide live GPS tracking links sent directly to your phone, along with action photos and video updates throughout the excursion.

What happens in bad weather conditions?

We walk in most weather, but if extreme conditions (such as severe thunderstorms, high heatwaves, or flash flooding) pose a safety hazard, we pivot to indoor enrichment, puzzle games, or shorter relief visits.

Do you offer meet-and-greets before booking?

Absolutely! We always prefer a brief introductory meeting with you and your dog to understand their personality, routines, dietary needs, and favorite treats before starting our regular walks.
